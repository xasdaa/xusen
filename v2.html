Storyline: On Halloween night, an Aosu full-color night-vision camera captures three neighborhood children in Halloween costumes arriving to trick-or-treat. As they reach the spot where the candy is set out, the child dressed as a mummy grabs one large lollipop. Just as he reaches for a second one, the child dressed as a vampire stops him—pointing to a sign that specifies only one treat per person. A little girl nearby witnesses this and laughs delightedly. Outside the frame of the phone screen, a giant skeletal hand appears, underscoring the Halloween theme. Meanwhile, a little ghost—"Mr. Spook"—is drifting along, wobbling as he goes, when a massive skeletal hand suddenly emerges in the middle of his path. Caught off guard, the ghost fails to dodge in time and crashes into the phone screen, left dazed and dizzy.
